Frequently asked questions

Before you reach out

Should I ask about a private autopsy right away?

If you are considering an examination, reach out promptly and share any scheduled funeral or cremation dates so timing and logistics can be discussed.

Can an existing autopsy be reviewed?

An inquiry can start with the original report and a list of the materials available. The scope depends on the question and whether original records, images, or specimens can be obtained.

Does every examination include toxicology?

Discuss testing during intake. The proposed scope can specify which tests are relevant and whether samples are available.

How are costs and report timing determined?

Ask for a case-specific estimate and anticipated timing. Costs and timing can depend on the examination, outside testing, facility and transport arrangements, and the requested deliverables.

How should I share records?

Start with a general inquiry. Request instructions for secure transfer before sharing reports, images, or other sensitive material.
